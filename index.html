How Moonvale plays

Your heroes fight on their own, even while you're away. You build the party, gear it up and push deeper. The deeper you go, the bigger the rewards.

Build your party

Up to 12 heroes from 5 classes. Warriors tank, Priests heal (max 2 of each), Mages, Archers and Thieves deal the damage. Founders field 15.

Clear the phases

The Meadow holds phases 1 to 10. Each phase has a clock of a few minutes (4 min 18 s at phase 1, up to 10 min): miss it and you restart at phase 1, keeping all your gear and cards. The clock keeps running while your party rests, and your party keeps hunting even with the game closed.

Bosses & Mythic cards

Every 5th phase ends with one of 10 bosses. Each can drop its own Mythic card (0.2% per kill) to socket into your gear.

Gear in 5 rarities

Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic. Ten slots per hero. Higher rarity, better stats and more card sockets.

Summon, fuse, rest

Summon new heroes with coins, fuse duplicates into stronger ones, and upgrade your House so your party recovers stamina faster.
