Get a presale kit

Presale kits

Kits come with Moonlit class skins: one skin dresses the whole class and gives +5% farm to every hero of that class, even the ones you summon later. Keep the look or just the bonus. Everything lands in your account the moment the game opens.

Kit heroes, gear and coins are locked to your account: you play with them, they can't be withdrawn. Farmed coins can be withdrawn after launch.

How the presale works

  1. 1
    Connect your Solana walletPhantom, Solflare, Backpack or MetaMask. You sign a message to prove the wallet is yours. No transaction, no fee.
  2. 2
    Pick a kit and pay in SOLYour wallet shows the exact amount before you approve. The purchase is confirmed on-chain.
  3. 3
    Play on launch dayConnect the same wallet on Saturday and your kit is already there.
Where the SOL goes
50% reserve50% team

Half of every sale goes to the reserve, which buys the coin at launch and backs player payouts. The reserve wallet is public, so anyone can check it.
