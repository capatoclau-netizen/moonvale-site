How Moonvale plays

Your heroes fight on their own, even while you're away. You build the party, gear it up and push deeper. The deeper you go, the bigger the rewards.

Build your party

Up to 12 heroes from 5 classes. Warriors tank, Priests heal (max 2 of each), Mages, Archers and Thieves deal the damage. Founders field 15.

Clear the phases

The Meadow holds phases 1 to 10. Each phase has a clock of a few minutes (4 min 18 s at phase 1, up to 10 min): miss it and you restart at phase 1, keeping all your gear and cards. The clock pauses while your party rests, and your party keeps hunting even with the game closed.

Bosses & Mythic cards

Every 5th phase ends with one of 10 bosses. Each can drop its own Mythic card (0.2% per kill) to socket into your gear.

Gear in 5 rarities

Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic. Ten slots per hero. Higher rarity, better stats and more card sockets.

Summon, fuse, rest

Summon new heroes with coins, fuse duplicates into stronger ones, and upgrade your House so your party recovers stamina faster.

Farm & withdraw

Every kill farms coins. Farmed coins can be withdrawn to your wallet (3% fee, once every 24 h). Payouts are capped by what the pool can afford, so it never runs dry.

Presale kits

Kits come with Moonlit class skins: one skin dresses the whole class and gives +5% farm to every hero of that class, even the ones you summon later. Keep the look or just the bonus. Everything lands in your account the moment the game opens.

Only in presale kits

🌙 Half of every kit buys the coin at launch. The Moonvale Vault buys on pump.fun the moment the coin goes live, so the game starts with real demand behind it.

Kit heroes, gear and coins are locked to your account: you play with them, they can't be withdrawn. Farmed coins can be withdrawn after launch.
