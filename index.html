How Moonvale plays

Your heroes fight on their own, even while you're away. You build the party, gear it up and push deeper. The deeper you go, the bigger the rewards.

Build your party

Up to 12 heroes from 5 classes: 1 Warrior tanks, 1 Priest heals, and Mages, Archers and Thieves deal the damage. Founders field 15.

Climb the floors

The floors go on past 100, and monsters get tougher from floor 50 and again from 75. Each floor has a clock (4 min 18 s on floor 1, up to 7 min): miss it or lose a fight and you restart at floor 1, keeping all your gear and cards. Your party keeps hunting even with the game closed.

Bosses & Mythic cards

Every 5th floor ends with one of 10 bosses. Each can drop its own Mythic card (0.05% per boss won) to socket into your gear.

Gear in 6 rarities

Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic. Ten slots per hero. Higher rarity, better stats and more card sockets.

Summon, fuse, rest

Summon new heroes with coins, fuse duplicates into stronger ones, and upgrade your House so your party recovers stamina faster.
